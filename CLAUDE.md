# Danmakusu

An osu! (lazer) ruleset: osu! standard maps played as a bullet-hell. Hit objects play themselves and fire bullets; the player dodges. Bosses (Touhou characters) take the map's breaks, kiai sections and spinners, each with a mechanic of their own.

## Build and deploy

- `dotnet build` builds and deploys. After the build the csproj closes osu! (asks it to close, kills it after 5 s) and copies the dll to `%APPDATA%\osu\rulesets`, and also copies it to `bin\Release\net10.0` (the build output itself stays in `bin\Debug\net10.0`, which the tools reference). Then `archive.ps1` zips the project folder (without bin/obj/.claude) plus the built dll into `..\Archives\Danmakusu-<date>-<time>.zip`, keeping the newest 30, and copies the latest to `..\Archives\osu.Game.Rulesets.Danmakusu.zip` and the dll to `..\Archives\osu.Game.Rulesets.Danmakusu.dll` (same names every build, replaced) (`-p:Archive=false` skips it). The shared zip on Google Drive (the second account, `<drive>:\<My Drive>\<shared folder>\Danmakusu`: G: now, it was I:; every drive is looked at, `-DriveRoot` names one, `-DriveFolder`) is overwritten in place too, so its link stays; nothing is created when it is not there.
- GitHub (since 2026-11-08, only when the user asks): public repo https://github.com/Begisso/Danmakusu with the code, the csproj, `Resources` and (since 2026-11-13) the notes CLAUDE.md / HISTORY.md / SKILLS.md (sync.ps1 replaces the Drive folder's name and the Windows user folder in them: write nothing else private into the notes), no `archive.ps1`, a README with the user's disclaimer (fan work, no license, no ownership claimed). Its copy is `..\GitHub\Danmakusu`, filled by `..\GitHub\sync.ps1`; commit there as Begisso with `56500027+Begisso@users.noreply.github.com`, push, and attach the built dll to a release (`gh release create vYYYY.MM.DD <dll> --notes-file <file>`: PowerShell 5.1 breaks notes with quotes). git and gh are portable in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\MinGit\cmd` and `...\GitHubCLI\bin` (no winget here); put them on `$env:Path` first.
- `dotnet build -p:CloseOsu=false` leaves osu! running: the copy then fails with a warning (useful as a compile check while the user is playing).
- Target: `net10.0`, `ppy.osu.Game` / `ppy.osu.Game.Rulesets.Osu` / `ppy.osu.Game.Rulesets.Mania` (since 2026-11-14, only `Bullets/ManiaNotes.cs` uses it: Yatsuhashi's 18-key convert by osu!mania's own converter; SectionCheck references it too). It must match the installed lazer (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\osulazer\current`; a compiled call to a method that changed is a `MissingMethodException` when a map loads, the 2026-11-18 crash). Since 2026-11-18 (lazer 2026.1117.0, whose game packages were not on NuGet yet) the ruleset, SectionCheck and LazerImport take `ppy.osu.Framework` at the installed version, the game's packages at the newest there is (2026.1026.0) for the libraries, and are compiled against the installed lazer's own `osu.Game*.dll` (`UseInstalledLazer` in the csproj; the tools also copy the lazer's dlls over theirs after the build). After an osu! update: SKILLS.md, "When osu! updates".
- There are no tests. Verify by building, then by playing in osu!.
- Not a git repo: there's no history except `HISTORY.md`.

## Where things are

- `DanmakusuBalance.cs`: every tunable constant (`NUE_*`, `TOYOHIME_*`, ...). Change numbers here rather than inline.
- `Bosses/BossTuning.cs`: values the player can adjust in the boss menu (`nue_pull`, `toyohime_turn`, ...). `DanmakusuBalance` reads some of them. Changing a default here may not reach a player who has already saved their own value.
- `Bosses/`: the roster (`BossRoster`), mechanics enum (`BossMechanic`; Nue is `Unknown`, Toyohime `FreeFall`, Junko `Purification`), descriptions shown in game (`BossDescriptions`), difficulty stars (`BossDifficulty`, one line per boss), colour matching.
- `Bullets/BulletPlanner.cs`: plans the whole map up front: rings (`BulletEmitter`), lasers, boss sections (`BossPeriod`, attack windows `LaserStarts`/`LaserEnds`). `planMechanics` applies boss mechanics that change or add rings. `Sections` / `BarPeriods` give the section layout without planning bullets.
- `UI/BulletField*.cs`: the runtime field (one partial class over many files). `UI/BulletField.Stage.cs` builds the per-boss stage acts in `buildStage`.
- `UI/Stages/*Act.cs`: one `StageAct` per boss mechanic that has things on the playfield (Nue `UfoAct`, Toyohime `FreeFallAct`, Sunny Milk `PrismAct`, Aunn `KomainuAct`, Momiji `PatrolAct`, Star Sapphire `SphereAct`, ...). They take, push or catch bullets and draw into `StageLayer`s. `CuttingAct` is the base of those that cut bullets in two (Kagerou and Elly `ClawAct`, Youmu `SlashAct`, Ran `FoxAct`). `WingRig` is Matenshi's wing as bones and feathers (each pixel the nearest one's; `sectioncheck wingrig` / `wingpose` draw it).
- `Bullets/BossOps.cs` + `Bullets/Trajectory.cs`: what bosses do to everything that flies, as ops a ring's or a beam's closed form goes through (Seija's `Bounce`, the freezes, since 2026-11-20 Kanako's `TurnBack` - `BossOnly`, for what bosses shoot - and Kokoro's `Aim` / `Pull` / `Gravity`, whose points her act places while playing: `Placed`; an act that places an op sets `autoOpsMoved`). Beams go by Gengetsu's clock only (`LaserEmitter.Clock` = `BossEffects.ForBeams`). Ops by every ring's own clock (since 2026-11-21: Rin's `LaterFreeze`, Aunn's `StopAndGo`) are templates the trace ignores: `BulletEmitter.ConfigureOps` (`withOwnTimers`) makes them into the ring's own freezes and aims; an act that places one ring's aim calls `autoRingMoved(ring)`, not `autoOpsMoved`. A look can swell the hitbox too (`BulletLook.HitScale`, `BulletEmitter.OpsGrowMax`); their warnings are drawn with the bullets (`UI/Stages/SwellWarnings.cs`). A beam whose only op is its own one bounce (Suwako's, Tsukasa's: `OnlyMarked`, `WholeBeams`) bounces in closed form (`LaserEmitter.bouncedOnce`, corners `bounceCorners`, gone at `BeamGoneAt`). A boss whose freeze meets Kokoro's fall goes through `BossOps.UnfreezeAt` (the planner too, for Cirno's bursts). Stage bullets can sway round a centre (`SwayAmp`, Yamame's web) or go round a moving hub (`Orbit`, Kanako's circles), still functions of time.
- `UI/StageLayer.cs`: sprite pools; procedural textures are baked here (`StageTexture`), and the seamless cracked-lava picture (`BakeLava`, since 2026-11-22) that the renderer draws into mesh pieces marked `MeshQuad.Lava` (Suika's cracks).
- The sound of being hit is the ruleset's own (`Resources/Samples/danmakusu_hit.wav`, the Touhou Boss Rush map's combo break, at every hit since 2026-11-22); the ruleset's skin transformer (`Bosses/BossColours.cs`) silences the skin's combo break sounds.
- A slow second in osu!'s log ("gameplay stood" lines) ends with the three acts that cost the most in it (`; acts <boss> <act> ms`, since 2026-11-22): look there first for a freeze the bench does not show.
- `UI/BossHud.cs`: the boss HP bar at the top.
- `UI/ImpactStarLayer.cs`: the World's Ender's impact frame, drawn as real shapes that turn what is under them inside out.
- `Bullets/StarRating.cs` + `DanmakusuDifficultyCalculator.cs`: the star rating (since 2026-11-08) combines bullet density (`Bullets/BulletDensity.cs`, `DENSITY_*`: how much of the playfield the bullets cut through per second) and movement to safer spots (`Bullets/ForcedMovement.cs`, `FORCED_*`: a lazy player plays the map without bosses; how far they walk, how often they start a dodge or must teleport): stars = `STAR_SCALE` x density ^ `STAR_DENSITY_POWER` x movement ^ `STAR_MOVEMENT_POWER`, fitted to osu!'s stars over the downloaded maps. (A rework fitted to dodging, 2026-11-14, was rolled back on 2026-11-15: the user found it too bad.) The boss menu shows the stars with the chosen bosses (`StarRating.RateWithBosses`). pp stays osu!'s.
- `DanmakusuBeatmapConverter.cs` + `Bullets/ConvertedModes.cs`: maps of the other modes (since 2026-11-08). Mania and taiko stand on a ring (twice as big in boss sections, `Arrange` in `DanmakusuPlayfield.buildField`), long notes and drumrolls are sliders that do not move (`Info.Static`, beams `LaserEmitter.HoldBeam`: tell a spinner's beams by `SpinnerLike`, not `Rotates`); taiko big notes shoot arc rings (`UI/BulletField.Drums.cs`); catch objects fall (`DanmakusuPlayfield.fallObjects`, `SliderZone.FallSpeed`) and shoot fans from the bottom and the top. Without the game the decoder drops a map's mode: tools call `ConvertedModes.KeepMode`. osu! offers only osu!standard maps to convert: `OtherModesInSongSelect.cs` patches that (Harmony, embedded) for Danmakusu alone; `sectioncheck offered` checks it. Never change the maps or how osu!'s own modes are offered.
- `Stars/`: song select by the Danmakusu stars (since 2026-11-08). `DanmakusuStars` keeps them by map MD5 in `danmakusu_stars.csv` in osu!'s data folder (never in osu!'s database); `StarFiller` (hidden in the ruleset icon) rates the library in the background; `SongSelectStars` (Harmony) makes the difficulty sort, the difficulty groups and the star filters use them in Danmakusu only, and adds the sorts "Bullets/Beams/Most bosses - Danmakusu" (SortMode 200-202; every difficulty on its own, as the sort by difficulty) to the dropdown. `DanmakusuBeatmap` (made by the converter) shows bullets, beams, spinners and most bosses in song select instead of circles and sliders; the counts are kept with the stars. In osu! a map not counted yet is counted by `CountsInBackground` ("…" until then); `SongSelectCounts` (Harmony) lays the four out as bars like osu!'s and shows them again when they come in. `Mods/DanmakusuModColumn.cs`: the "Danmakusu" column of the mod screen (Harmony; the mods stay Difficulty Reduction to osu!, and since 2026-11-15 Danmakusu's Autoplay is shown there too, taken out of the Automation column - it stays an Automation mod to osu!). Raise `StarRating.STORE_VERSION` whenever the stars come out differently (formula, `STAR_*`/`DENSITY_*`/`FORCED_*`, or what the planner makes of a map without bosses), or song select keeps sorting by the old ratings. `sectioncheck sortstars` checks the patches; `sectioncheck counts <map.osu> [...]` prints what song select shows of a map.
- `Mods/`: boss selection mods (one, two or three bosses at once: `BS`, `DS`, `TS`; rank 0, 1, 2 in `BossPeriod.Rank`), the boss picker (with its HP bar preview, the boss count and the attacks-per-boss slider, value `count;attacks|ids`) and the pre-map boss menu. When a map starts the gameplay clock is held until the game draws smoothly (`DanmakusuPlayfield.StartHold`, in osu!'s gameplay clock container; the boss menu hands its Start to it), so a long first frame does not eat into the map. `AlwaysBoss.cs` (since 2026-11-13): Always Boss (`AB`: marks the map, `SectionMarks`; `BulletPlanner.alwaysBossSections`). `FocusLens.cs` (since 2026-11-21): Focus Lens (`FS`; no zoom since 2026-11-22): the camera on the character (its ghost while frozen or stunned) - the playfield, as it always looks, moves round it - only the look: the cursor is put where the mouse is without the view, as for Seija's turn (`DanmakusuPlayfield.updateMirror` / `updateFocus`); the edges of the field (the screen as it is without the mod) a white line, black past them (`UI/FocusFrame.cs`, a layer under the boss HUD's proxy); the field works the screen out without the view (`BulletField.ViewShift`/`ViewScale`, `unview`: anything that measures the screen through the playfield must undo it, or it moves with the camera); the boss HUD undoes the view (`BossHud.SetView`). (Map Generation, 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-15, was removed: the user, "Experiment failed".)
- `Bullets/SongBands.cs`: the song's loudness in three bands and its drum hits, which the section picking listens to. Its times are real ms (`MsPerPoint`: osu!'s waveform points are 0.9977 ms for 44.1 kHz songs) less `hit_lag_ms`; tools read songs with `SongBands.FromFile`.
- `UI/BulletField.AutoPilot.cs` + `Mods/DanmakusuModAutoplay.cs` (since 2026-11-13): Danmakusu's own Autoplay (osu!'s is replaced in this ruleset). Its replay is a still cursor; the field moves the character like the star rating's lazy player on the real field: stands still while nothing would touch it within `AUTO_HORIZON_MS`, walks the shortest safe way (keyboard speed with keyboard controls), teleports when no walk is safe. It foresees rings, stage bullets, beams, star tails (since 2026-11-15; a hit by one is counted as a beam's), moon stars, circles, sliders and every act's `Hurts`; an act whose own hazards (not stage bullets) are worked out step by step must answer for the time to come too (Reimu's orbs, `SealAct.orbAhead`; what homes, on `realCharacterPosition`); a hazard that goes off once inside an act's update must answer `StageAct.Springs` too, or the autoplay walks into it; a stage bullet that the act moves step by step in its update (a trail that grows: Koakuma's bats, Nazrin's arrowheads) is seen flying straight on from the end of its trail unless the act answers `StageAct.ForeseePath` (2026-11-15: seen standing still before, the bats' 14 hits); beads are asked for at every moment the autoplay looks at (`Beads(f, t, ...)`), so an act whose beads move must place them at `t`, not where they are now (Hecatia's chains are stepped on ahead on copies, `ensureChainAhead`, her thrown worlds too, `ensureAhead`); an act that limits where the character can be (Eiki's circles) must put the autoplay's paths through the limit too (`JudgementAct.Held` in `autoWay`), or it walks out, is held where it did not look, and is hit (the 2026-11-14 fail). What it foresees of fired rings is kept from frame to frame on a grid of moments (`RingTrack`: a ring changed at runtime other than by a deflection is not noticed for up to 400 ms); its search for a way out gets `AUTO_BUDGET_MS` a frame, counted from when the search starts, and always looks at the nearest 32 teleport places (no setting, the user's choice). It asks every act's `Hurts` hundreds of times a frame at a few moments: an act whose `Hurts` costs (long lists of beams or bullets) keeps its shapes per moment with `MomentShapes` (BulletField.Stage.cs; Satori), at a grid of moments kept for a few frames with `GridShapes` while `autoAsking` (Ringo, Sekibanki/Byakuren: made every moment of every frame they cost 5 ms a frame) or puts boxes round them (Wakasagihime). `AUTO_ACTS=1` on the bench prints what its questions cost every act. Check with the bench (`AUTO_PILOT=1`; every boss alone with `singles`: an autoplay line with "ways" over 1 ms a frame is an act to look at).

## Tools

Step-by-step procedures (build, benchmark, section tuning, spreadsheet, images) are in `SKILLS.md`.

- `..\Tools\SectionCheck`: loads a map and its audio the way the game does, prints the boss sections the game would choose and how well every section-scoring measure separates a list of wanted sections from the rest. `dotnet run --project ..\Tools\SectionCheck -- "<map.osu>" "<audio>" out.csv "0:48-1:07,1:36-2:34"`. Build the ruleset first (it references `bin\Debug\net10.0`). Use it before changing the section weights.
  - `maps.ps1` lists the user's example maps with their wanted sections (`$a = & .\maps.ps1 -All`); add new examples there. `auc` shows how well each measure separates on every map, `eval "<weights>" "2/0.9"` shows what one weight set picks, `climb "-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4" 400` hill-climbs all measures (best for many measures).
  - `field "<map.osu>" m:ss <seconds> singles|pairs|pairs:a,b,c|a+b,c+d|a+b+c` (three names: all three at once) runs the real `BulletField.Update` headlessly for those boss combinations and prints ms per frame, the costliest steps of `Update` (`BulletField.ProfileTicks`) and live rings/bullets/beams. Use it first for freezes; ids can be shortened (`reisen`). See SKILLS.md.
  - `AUTO_PILOT=1` on `field`: the autoplay plays (`AUTO_KEYS=1` with keyboard controls); `BENCH_STILL=1`: the player stands still (at x 256, or `BENCH_STILL_X`; the field is the whole screen, about -170..682). Both print the hits by cause; the autoplay also what it costs. `HIT_TIMES=1` prints every hit with its time; `BENCH_FRAME_MS=40` makes every frame at least that long (the game's frames draw too: at low fps the autoplay costs more and dodges worse); `AUTO_BUDGET=0.01` starves the autoplay's budget, as a slower machine does; `RING_COUNTS=1` prints the live rings every second, to compare with a log's slow-second lines; `LASER_LIST=1` prints the first beams planned (when, speed, lifetime); the autoplay's search budget is real time, so runs differ: compare several runs, not one, or make them alike with `AUTO_BUDGET=100`; `CUT_PROBE=1` prints every cut made near the character; `HIT_PROBE=1` (with `HIT_TIMES=1`) says, for a hit by a ring's bullet, where the bullet and the character were swept from and to and where the bullet flew before; `AUTO_ACTS=1` what the autoplay's questions cost every act; the bench has no playfield that turns with Seija's screen (`NothingTurnsProbe`); `ALWAYS_BOSS=1` (also on `periods`) plays the map as Always Boss marks it (a log does not say whether it was on: bosses before the first section of a plain game, and ring counts that drop at the same seconds, tell).
  - `long` (sixth argument of `field`): every piece of a beam longer than 60 % of the screen, the first ones printed with their laser, and (since 2026-11-21) the acts' and tails' long pieces with who drew them (`BulletField.PlainBeamProbe`); `LONG_TIMES=1` counts them every five seconds by kind of beam and its ops. Beams whose neighbouring slices went wholly different ways are cut (`LaserEmitter.cutStretched`, `BEAM_CUT_*`): the "infinite laserbeams" of 2026-11-21.
  - `PORTAL_LENGTH=1` on `field` (since 2026-11-22; with `long`, so the portals move): every run of a beam that has been through Okina's portals against the same slices traced without them - the user's rule is that a beam through a portal keeps its length, so "stretched" must be 0. It reads the points as the beam makes them (`LaserEmitter.PointsProbe`: which beam, and when each slice was fired). Only the low 16 bits of a look's `Portal` are portals; the beam's own cuts count above them.
  - `EARLY_GONE=1` on `field` (since 2026-11-22): a once-bouncing beam that is ended while a point of it is still on the screen (by its gone time, its time being up, or as flying away); the first frame's clean-up of old beams far off the screen shows too.
  - `EDGE_PROBE=1` on `field`: every bullet that comes into the screen from outside it, whose it is, how fast (times a plain bullet) and whether it flies at the player, and the most round bullets drawn sharp in a frame. With `ACT_PROBE=1` and an old copy of the exe it shows what a change did to every mechanic.
  - `convert <map.osu> [...]`: a map of any mode: what the decoder makes of its objects, what the game converts them into, where the first ones stand, and the rings and beams the planner makes.
  - `periods "<map.osu>" "a+b" [double|triple]` prints the boss sections planned for those bosses, with rank, kind, revivals and the pieces Yorihime repeats (with Yatsuhashi: her falling notes per section and the whole 18-key convert).
  - `breaks <map.osu> <audio or -> [...]` lists every section and break of a map, chosen as the game does (from the song too; `-` for no song), and every section still shorter than an attack and a pause.
  - `averages "1,0.95" <map.osu> <audio> <targets or -> [...]` shows, per map, how much of the song the tuned score alone, the game (the score with its floor, `PickWithFloor`) and the averages of hitsounds and song volume would cover, and how wrong each is against the wanted sections.
  - `lengths <map.osu> [...]` plans every map with as many bosses as it has room for and counts the attacks and pauses under the default length, with plain bosses and with the longest ones. `fit` and this are the checks after any change to the sections.
  - `looks "<map.osu>" <boss id>` counts the bullets on the playfield by drawn shape around the first pauses of that boss (before, during, after): checks what an op turns bullets into.
  - `shinkiwing <wing.png> <pairs.png>` (since 2026-11-22) draws Shinki's wing as `StageLayer.makeShinkiWing` makes it, and her three pairs round her at rest; `lava <out.png>` the lava picture (`StageLayer.BakeLava`) twice side by side, to see that it is seamless.
  - `stars <map.osu> [...]` prints the movement star rating (`Bullets/ForcedMovement.cs`, no bosses) next to osu!'s and the old density's for every map, with walked distance, dodges and teleports, and the scale/power that fit osu!'s best (`$a = & .\maps.ps1 -All` works as the list).
  - `alwaysboss <map.osu>` prints the sections with and without Always Boss and how many bosses fit with 1-4 attacks each; `ratetime <map.osu>` times the star rating part by part.
  - `starlist "<folder of .osu>" <out.csv>` rates every osu!standard map of a folder (on every core, ~0.25 s a map) with the game's own Danmakusu calculator (no mods, and DT) next to osu!'s stars; maps already in the csv are skipped. The downloaded maps' list is `H:\osu maps\danmakusu_stars.csv`, kept up to date by `Tools\LazerImport\watch.ps1` from its own copy `bin\Stars` (`dotnet build -c Release -o bin\Stars`).
  - `bench "<map.osu>" "boss_id,boss_id" m:ss <seconds>` plans a map with those bosses (both in every section) and times the per-frame bullet work the way the field keeps rings alive. Use it for performance reports ("the game freezes with X").
  - `search` mode tries every combination of the section weights on several maps at once and lists the best: `dotnet run -c Release --project ..\Tools\SectionCheck -- search "0,1,2" "<map1.osu>" "<audio1>" "<targets1>" "<map2.osu>" ...`. Steps "0,1,2,3" over 9 measures take about 3 minutes for 6 maps. The current weights came from Chirality + Cyaegha (see HISTORY.md, Boss sections); add the user's new example maps to the search rather than tuning on one map, or the other maps break.
- `..\Tools\RefCheck` (since 2026-11-18): `refcheck <dll> <folder it runs with> [more dlls]` looks up every type, method and field a dll takes from other assemblies, by name and signature, in the dlls of a folder (the installed lazer, with Harmony's dll for the ruleset): what it prints as MISSING is a `MissingMethodException` waiting in the game. `refcheck names <folder> "Type::member" ...` checks names that the ruleset looks up by reflection.
- `..\Tools\BossPictures` (since 2026-11-22): the small pictures of a new boss from her art - `sprite` (Sprites, 172 x 193), `crop` (Heads, 96 x 96), `inpaint`/`clearsoft`/`clear` (the yellow scribbles out of ZUN's art), `grid`/`pixel`/`sheet`/`info` to look; the whole procedure is SKILLS.md, "New characters".
- `..\Tools\Portraits`: makes the big pictures of the boss description (`Resources/Bosses/Portraits/<id>.webp`) from the full art in `old builds and assets\Touhou Characters`. `dotnet run --project ..\Tools\Portraits -- "<art folder>" Resources\Bosses\Sprites Resources\Bosses\Portraits [ids]`; `... sheet <portraits> out.png <ids> [cell]` makes a page to look at. The in-game sprites stay small.
- `..\Tools\Bake`: makes the sounds and pictures that were not made by hand. `earth <picture> Resources\Textures 1536 14 25 1810 UI\Stages\EarthPieces.cs <preview.png>` bakes the World's Ender's Earth, its chunks and its halves (the line of the cut in it must stay `WorldEnderAct.cutProfile`); `boat <picture> Resources\Textures\futo_boat.png 4 <preview.png> pixel` cuts Futo's ship out of a checkerboard background (its outline is also the boat's hit and push shape, `StageLayer.BoatOutline`; `pixel` enlarges pixel art with Scale2x before Lanczos, so it stays sharp), `wings` cuts Matenshi's pair of wings out of a white background, splits it and prints the shoulder, aspect and feather spots (`StageLayer.WING_ROOT`/`WING_ASPECT`/`WING_SPOTS`), `feather` levels and cuts out a feather and traces its outline (`DanmakusuBalance.FEATHER`, `FEATHER_ASPECT`), `fumo <photo> <out.png> <largest px> <preview.png> [least white] [step] [shadow from]` cuts a photo out of a white background without eating into white parts behind darker edges, and takes the shadow below a height too (Alice's fumo: `238 10 0.86`), `stereo` balances and raises a stretch of a sound, `blast` takes music out from under an explosion, `clean` keeps a voice by its pitch, `look` prints the loudness and draws a spectrogram of a wav, `wav` decodes.

## Conventions

- Match the existing comment style: plain sentences describing what happens in the game, asides in `(parentheses)`, a header comment block at the top of each act.
- Everything must be deterministic for replays and seeking: choose bullets by index, not at random, and make acts survive time going backwards (`Reset`).
- A ring holds at most 64 bullets (`MAX_RING_BULLETS`, 64-bit hit masks); split bigger ones.
- When a boss's behaviour changes, update its line in `BossDescriptions.cs` too, and its stars in `BossDifficulty.cs` if it got easier or harder. A new boss needs a line in both.

## Working with the user

- The user tunes bosses with short instructions and often revises earlier ones. Read the boss's section of `HISTORY.md` first so a change builds on the latest state.
- After every change, add an entry to `HISTORY.md` (per boss, per date, old → new values) and run `dotnet build` to deploy.
- Whenever a placeholder boss gets a mechanic of its own: no laser beams in the spinner, and the spinner's stars are replaced by the boss's own bullets (see `planSection` in `BulletPlanner.cs`).
- When an instruction can be read more than one way, pick the likeliest reading, say which one you took, and offer the alternative.
